Rate is a range. Shown as a range because HD Saison's own published APR spans a wide band by product, and a single figure invites correction from anyone in the industry. Say the word if you'd rather force one number per lender.

Satisfaction needs its scale. Rendered as 3.5 / 5. Tell me the scale you're using and where it came from — this is the only one of the three that a reader can't sanity-check from experience, so it leans hardest on the source line.

Deltas. The three new rows carry no "vs HD Saison" delta line. Rate and time only compare meaningfully as a range against a range, and a delta on a satisfaction score reads as false precision. Easy to add if you disagree.

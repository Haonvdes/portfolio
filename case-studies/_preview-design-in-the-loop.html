Workflow model

Design-in-the-Loop

A normal git-flow — main → dev → feature — running alongside a design track of its own. Tokens and components are long-lived branches design and engineering both pull from. A concept branch is short-lived: spun up per feature, pushed hard with AI during discovery, and either merged in or closed. Click any commit below for what actually happens at that point.

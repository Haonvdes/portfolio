The problem statement

HD Saison originated 100% of its loans in-branch — the only lender in its five-company peer set with no digital path at all, in a market growing 23.7% a year (published)

Business pain point

1

Four-stage approval

Borrowing took 3 to 5 hours across four stages, every one of them in person (client-reported).

2

Eight signatures

Closing a single loan took eight separate wet-ink signatures (client-reported).

3

Drop-off at the counter

Borrowers gave up mid-application, and nothing was instrumented to show where.

4

Falling repeat-borrow rate

The repeat-borrow rate was quietly falling, and nobody could say why.

Business goal

1

E-signature

Replace eight wet-ink signatures with one binding electronic one.

2

Loan Management

One place to see loan balance, payments and due dates.

3

Payment Integration

Repay a loan without going back to a dealer or branch.

4

Remarketing System

Re-engage past borrowers for their next loan.

Outcomes / Success metric

5h → 2h Approval time, end to end (target)
8 → 1 Signatures to take out a loan (client-reported)

The problem

HD Saison originated 100% of its loans in-branch — four in-person stages, eight wet-ink signatures, three to five hours per loan — and it was the only lender in its five-company peer set with no digital path at all.

What I decided

I reordered the client's four equal-weight goals to put electronic signing first, because nothing downstream removes a branch visit that exists to collect eight signatures — and killed remarketing outright.

What changed

The app shipped in May 2022 and electronic signing is in HD Saison's production flow today; approval time was targeted at two hours, down from five, and I left before anyone measured it.

What we built

Electronic signing Loan management Payment integration Remarketing — cut, and why is further down
